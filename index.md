# Keyworks: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026. Sycamore Hill Studios ("we") makes Keyworks: Idle Dungeon RPG ("the game"). This page says what data the game handles and why.

## No accounts or servers of our own

The game has no sign-up of its own and we don't run a game server. Your progress (heroes, gear, chests, keys, upgrades, event and season progress, purchases already delivered, and settings) is saved on your device. If Android backup is on, Android may copy that save to your Google Account and restore it on a new phone; this is run by Google and we can't see it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes the copy on your device.

## Google Play Games

If you use Google Play Games, the game signs in with your Play Games profile automatically. Google then stores, under your Play Games profile: your achievements, your scores on the game's leaderboards (deepest floor, World Boss, events and others), and a cloud copy of your save ("Saved Games") so you can continue on another device. Leaderboards show your Play Games name and avatar to other players according to your Play Games visibility settings. You can delete the game's cloud save in the game (Settings > Delete cloud save) and manage or delete your Play Games data in the Google Play Games app or at [myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com). See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Ads (Google AdMob)

The game only shows an ad when you tap a button that offers a stated reward for watching one, for example to double the gold you earned while away. There are no banner ads and no ads that start by themselves. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects and shares data to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud: your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), how you interact with ads and the app, and diagnostic data. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices, which is also where players in US states with privacy laws can opt out of personalized ads. We ask Google for ads rated for teens and below. See [how Google uses data from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. If you own the Patron's License, the game doesn't load the ads SDK at all.

## Game statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To find bugs and balance the game, the game uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They receive: how the game is used (for example, which floor you reached, which screens you opened, and which purchases succeeded or failed, without payment details), crash reports and device diagnostics (device model, Android version, memory), and an app instance identifier. This data is used only to improve the game. It is turned off for players that Google Play reports as under 18, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings > Privacy choices > Game statistics. Firebase also provides "Remote Config" so we can switch features or adjust events without an app update; it receives an app instance identifier.

## Purchases (Google Play)

Gems, bundles, passes, cosmetics, permanent upgrades and the Keyworks Club subscription are bought through Google Play. The game receives a record of each purchase (item, order number and purchase token) so it can deliver it and restore it later; that record stays on your device and in your Play Games cloud save. Google Play gives us sales reports for accounting. We never see your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment.

## Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example, when your vault is full or an event starts), at most two a day and none at night. They are created on the device; nothing is sent to us. You can turn each type off in the game's Settings or in Android's settings.

## Other Google Play features

- **Ratings:** the game may show Google Play's rating card. A rating or review you leave goes to Google Play under your Google Account; the game doesn't receive it.
- **Updates:** the game asks Google Play whether a newer version is available.
- **Age signals:** where the law requires it, Google Play may tell the game an age range for your account. The game uses it only on your device to make the game stricter for younger players (for example: no ads for players under 13, ads with Google's settings for minors, no paid random items and no game statistics for players under 18). It is never used for advertising, profiling or analytics and is never sent to us.
- **Time check:** to run daily resets and events fairly, the game reads the current time from a Google web server's response header. No personal data is sent.

## What we don't do

We don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. There is no chat and no way to contact other players inside the game. We don't sell personal data.

## Your rights and deleting your data

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to withdraw consent and to opt out of targeted advertising. The quickest tools are in the game: Settings > Privacy choices (ads consent and game statistics) and Settings > Delete cloud save. Uninstalling the game deletes the data on your device. Your advertising ID can be reset in Android settings, and Google's ad settings are at [myadcenter.google.com](https://myadcenter.google.com). For anything else, email us and we'll answer within 30 days.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Changes

We'll update this page and its date when the game changes, and tell you in the game before a significant change takes effect.
